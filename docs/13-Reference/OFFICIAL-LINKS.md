## Verification before creating accounts
1. Confirm Cloudflare Pages free tier, builds and policies.
2. Confirm Supabase database/storage/auth/Edge Function/free inactivity policies.
3. Confirm Gemini model IDs, pricing, quotas and whether free-tier data processing/privacy terms fit uploaded customer PDFs.
4. Confirm payment service onboarding and recurring-payment legal requirements for your registered merchant country.
5. Confirm country-specific data protection and consumer cancellation/refund requirements with a qualified adviser.
